Privacy policy

QNAP Drive Backup is a personal app. Its only user is its owner, and it only accesses the owner's own Google Drive.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google's read-only Drive access (the drive.readonly scope) to list and download the owner's Google Drive files.

How the data is used and stored

Files are copied into a backup on the owner's own NAS at home. The app does not change or delete anything in Google Drive.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone else, and none of it is used for advertising or to train AI models.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
